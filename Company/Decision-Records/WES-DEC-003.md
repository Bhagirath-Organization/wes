## Decision Summary
The Founder ratifies **`FOUNDER-INTENT` v1.0** (doc 03) and **`COMPANY-PHILOSOPHY` v1.0**
(doc 04) as governed external documents under Constitution §12 — retrieved by every AI Employee
before significant decisions.

## Reason
- Both documents are the Founder's own words, committed verbatim and reviewed/approved by the
  Founder (batch-1, PR #3).
- They are the two §12 external documents the Constitution depends on (Founder Intent and
  Company Philosophy); AI Employees require ratified versions to align decisions against.

## Alternatives Considered
- **Leave both as Draft.** Rejected — AI Employees must align to ratified governed documents,
  not drafts.
- **Ratify in a standalone PR.** Rejected — the Founder directed that this ratification be
  bundled into Batch 2's first PR rather than opened separately.

## Final Decision
`FOUNDER-INTENT` **v1.0** and `COMPANY-PHILOSOPHY` **v1.0** are **Ratified**, effective
2026-08-04 (the batch-1 documents were merged to `main` via PR #3, squash `43bb56b`). The
`INVENTORY` is updated to mark docs 03–04 **Ratified**.

## Impact
- Governed homes: `Company/Operating-Instructions/FOUNDER-INTENT.md` and
  `Company/Operating-Instructions/COMPANY-PHILOSOPHY.md`.
- These are now the authoritative §12 references for Founder Intent alignment and company values.
- No code, no deployment — production remains held for the combined end-of-phase deploy.
